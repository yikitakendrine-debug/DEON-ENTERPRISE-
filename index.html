DEON Enterprise is a full-service Design & Printing Company based at Zebra House Level 1, Nasser Road, Kampala. Process Transparency

MVO System Flow - How Your Order Moves From Idea To Delivery

Our refined, fail-safe 6-step production cycle guarantees speed, zero errors, and complete peace of mind.

01 Step 1

Customer Sends Order

Submit through our online form, direct WhatsApp (+256701099816), or visit our counter at Zebra House, Nasser Road.

02 Step 2

Design & Quote

Our graphic artists review your concept, prepare a Free Sample / Digital Mockup, and send the exact price in 10 minutes.

03 Step 3

Approve & Pay 50%

Approve proof, pay 50% deposit smoothly via MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, Bank Transfer, or Cash at the counter.

04 Step 4

Printing / Production

Production on high-speed Japanese printing engines and precision cutters followed by strict multi-point Quality Checks.

05 Step 5

Delivery / Installation

Same-day Boda / Courier delivery across Kampala, bus parcel dispatch upcountry, and expert on-site sign installation.

06 Step 6

Happy Client

Final client verification, 5-star review, warranty protection, and repeat business partnership with VIP loyalty perks.
